## Sections

1. Fixed header: wordmark, brand and creator menus, Portfolio & Reviews, Contact pill, SwayAI. Mobile uses a dialog menu.
2. Centered hero on `#fafafa` with a coral radial wash. Cormorant Garamond headline, Inter body. Stats count to 500+, 300+, and 50+ and can be replayed.
3. Logo marquee: Movals, Reliance Gulf, PSN, Razorpay, Zuptek. Loops in 15s unless reduced motion is on.
4. Two path cards with inline diagrams and overview films (34s brands, 32s creators). Posters switch to portrait below 650px. Play opens a player with captions and the narration transcript.
5. Service groups for brands and creators.
6. Portfolio & Reviews links out to swayhousereviews.in.
7. Dark closing panel with brief, management, email, and WhatsApp actions.
8. Footer contacts, legal links, and a short cookie-settings dialog.

## Type and color

- Paper `#fafafa`, ink `#1a1a1a`, muted `#565651`, coral `#ff6b35`, coral text `#b63d12`
- Display: Cormorant Garamond. UI: Inter.
- Shell `min(1180px, 100% - 80px)`, narrowing to `100% - 40px` under 1000px.
- Nav hides under 1000px. Path, service, portfolio, and closing columns stack under 800px.
